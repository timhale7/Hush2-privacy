# Privacy Policy for Hush!

**Effective date:** November 13, 2026  
**Application:** Hush! (`com.halesoftware.hush2`)  
**Developer:** [Google Play developer name]

### Overview

Hush! is an Android application that allows users to manage device volume levels, configure Quiet and Default volume profiles, and schedule periods of reduced sound.

Hush! is designed to operate locally on your device without requiring a user account or connection to a developer-operated server.

### Information Accessed and Stored

To provide its functionality, Hush! accesses and manages Android audio settings, including media, call, ringtone, notification, and alarm volume levels.

The application stores information locally on your device, including:

- User-configured volume profiles.
- Scheduled and manually initiated Hush sessions.
- Application preferences and configuration.
- Operational status, diagnostic information, and recovery records needed for reliable volume restoration.

This information is used to operate the application and maintain reliable scheduling and restoration behavior.

### Data Collection and Sharing

Hush! does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information to the developer.

The application does not require an account, display advertisements, or use developer-operated analytics or tracking services.

Hush! does not sell user information or share application data with advertisers.

### Permissions and Device Services

Hush! uses Android system capabilities to adjust volume settings, schedule operations, display notifications, and maintain reliable operation when the application is not visible.

These capabilities are used to perform the functions requested by the user.

### Data Export and Backup

Hush! allows users to export and import application settings and export diagnostic information.

These operations are initiated by the user. Exported files are saved to locations selected by the user and may be subject to the privacy and security practices of any external storage or cloud service the user chooses.

Android system backup or device-transfer services may also handle eligible application data according to the user's device settings and Google's applicable policies.

### Data Security

Application data is stored using Android's application storage mechanisms and device-level security protections.

Hush! does not operate a developer-hosted database containing users' volume profiles, schedules, or diagnostic records.

Users are responsible for protecting any files they export from the application.

### Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored settings and diagnostic information are retained as needed for application operation.

Users can remove locally stored application data through Android's application settings or by uninstalling the application, subject to Android backup and restoration behavior.

Files previously exported by the user must be deleted separately from their chosen storage locations.

Hush! does not maintain user accounts or a developer-hosted repository of user information requiring a separate account-deletion request.

### Children's Privacy

Hush! is intended for adult users and is not specifically directed toward children.

### Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when application functionality or data-handling practices change. Updates will be published at this policy's web address with a revised effective date.
